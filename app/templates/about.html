Independent Speakers Only 15 speakers (8 dysarthric, 7 healthy). 55 independent human subjects (31 dysarthric, 24 healthy).
Evaluation Split Random utterance split (caused speaker identity memorization). Strict Group Split by Speaker (Zero speaker overlap across train, val, and test).

4. Clinical Boundaries & Responsible AI Usage

Motor speech disorders have complex causes and require professional assessment. Acoustic classification research may provide useful hypotheses, but this prototype has not been validated for screening:

  • This system is for academic demonstration only and is not intended for clinical screening or triage.
  • False negatives may occur in individuals with mild dysarthria who preserve articulatory compensation.
  • Professional diagnosis requires comprehensive clinical evaluation including cranial nerve examination, acoustic vowel space analysis, and perceptual speech assessments by qualified medical professionals.
