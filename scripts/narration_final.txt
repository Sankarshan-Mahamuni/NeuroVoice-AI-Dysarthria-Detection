Welcome to NeuroVoice AI, an open-source clinical screening platform for detecting dysarthria from voice recordings. Our system achieves 87.4 percent test accuracy and 92.2 percent specificity on unseen human speakers with zero data leakage.

In our Knowledge Center, the Patient Guide explains how motor nerve conditions impair the four speech subsystems: respiration, phonation, articulation, and prosody.

The Subtypes tab maps all six clinical dysarthria variants to their underlying anatomical lesion sites and acoustic traits, from flaccid and spastic to ataxic and hypokinetic speech.

The Biomarkers tab details our 144 extracted features, including MFCCs, pitch perturbation, jitter, shimmer, and vocal tract formant dynamics.

The Benchmarks tab compares five machine learning models, demonstrating peak generalization with our regularized classifier against Random Forests and Neural Networks.

The Diagnostic Flow tab illustrates our end-to-end signal pipeline, from 16-kilohertz audio ingestion and silence trimming to feature scaling, classification, and clinical triage.

The Clinical Protocols tab details standardized diagnostic speech drills, including vowel prolongation, alternating syllables, and standardized passage reading.

Below, our Interactive 5-Point Symptom Screener allows clinicians to check observed vocal impairments, updating the live risk meter in real time.
