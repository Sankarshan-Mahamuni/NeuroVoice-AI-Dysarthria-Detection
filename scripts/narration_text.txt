Welcome to NeuroVoice AI, an open-source clinical screening platform for detecting dysarthria and speech disorders from voice recordings.

At the top, we display our verified benchmarks: an 87.4 percent test accuracy and 92.2 percent specificity on unseen human speakers with zero data leakage.

Let us explore our Knowledge Center. The Simplified Patient Guide explains how neurological conditions impair respiration, phonation, articulation, and prosody.

In the Subtypes tab, we examine the six clinical dysarthria variants, including Flaccid, Spastic, Ataxic, and Hypokinetic speech, mapped to their anatomical lesions and acoustic signs.

The Biomarkers tab presents the 144 acoustic features extracted by our engine, covering 40 MFCCs, log-Mel energies, pitch perturbation, jitter, shimmer, and formant dynamics.

The Benchmarks tab compares five machine learning models, demonstrating how our regularized classifier achieved peak generalization against Random Forests and Neural Networks.

The Diagnostic Flow tab diagrams our end-to-end signal pipeline, from 16-kilohertz resampling to clinical classification.

The Clinical Protocols tab details standardized diagnostic speech drills, such as vowel prolongation and the Rainbow Passage.

Below, our Interactive 5-Point Symptom Screener lets clinicians check observed vocal impairments, updating the live risk meter in real time.

Now, let us test the Acoustic Diagnosis Engine. When we analyze a healthy speech recording, the system confirms Normal Phonation with high confidence, rendering a clean, stable formant spectrogram.

When we test a dysarthric recording, the algorithm instantly detects acoustic instability, raising a Pathological Markers alert alongside a detailed clinical report and export options.

NeuroVoice AI provides fast, private, and reproducible speech disorder screening.
